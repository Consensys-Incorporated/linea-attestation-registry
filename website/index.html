Your Reputation under Your Control

Verax is a public registry for storing on-chain attestations, designed for enhanced discoverability and composability

Explore the Ecosystem

Unlocking the Value of Reputation

Verax allows dapps to easily access attestations from numerous issuers without having to integrate with each one directly. Unlike NFTs, attestations are easily discoverable and can be easily linked together. Users can see all their attestations in one place and discover the value they unlock.

For Users

  • Collect attestations within the registry in order to build up your reputation, and unlock its value
  • Tap into a diverse ecosystem of attestation issuers
  • Attestations you acquire can be used by any dapps across the ecosystem
  • Seamlessly link attestations together to compose your own on-chain profile
  • Revoke or replace any attestation whenever you want to

For Dapps

  • Filter out sybils and bots in an inclusive and user friendly way
  • Gain insights into your customer base and the users of the network
  • Offer promos, discounts and rewards to users of specific attestations
  • Source any type of data you need from many attestation issuers
  • Issue your own attestations that are easily discovered and used by any dapp across the network
